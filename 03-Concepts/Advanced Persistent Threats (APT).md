## Definition 
A sophisticated, continuous cyberattack where an intruder gains unauthorized access to a company's network and remains undetected for an extended period. 
## Real-world impact — SolarWinds attack (2020) 
State-sponsored attackers infiltrated SolarWinds, whose network management software was used by U.S. government agencies and Fortune 500 companies. They inserted malicious code into a routine update; installing clients unknowingly introduced the malware into their own systems (a **supply chain attack**). Allowed spying, data theft, and months of undetected access.
