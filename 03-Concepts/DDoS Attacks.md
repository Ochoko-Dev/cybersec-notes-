## Definition
Flooding the normal functioning of a website, server, or network with internet traffic from multiple sources simultaneously — a **botnet** (an amplification network of compromised devices). 
## How it works 
Three components: **attacker**, **botnet**, **victim**. 
1. Attacker sends commands to the botnet. 
2. Compromised devices send requests to the victim simultaneously. 
3. Traffic surge consumes the target's bandwidth and processing capacity. 
4. Target slows down or crashes. 
5. Legitimate users experience delays or outages. 
## Real-world impact — Dyn attack (2016) 
Targeted Dyn, a provider of critical internet infrastructure. Affected Twitter, Netflix, Reddit and others for hours across the US and Europe. Used the **Mirai botnet**, built from compromised IoT devices — see [[Domains of Information Security]]
