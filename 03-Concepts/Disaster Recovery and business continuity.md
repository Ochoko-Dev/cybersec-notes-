## Disaster Recovery (DR) 
Restoring the critical parts of a business that have failed so operations can continue with minimal interruption — recovering systems and data after a cyberattack or natural disaster. 
## Role of pen testers - 
Help identify vulnerabilities that could compromise DR and BC. 
- Test the effectiveness of recovery procedures.
