# Log Management

Provides access to all logs in an environment (web logs, OS logs, firewall, proxy, EDR, etc.) and allows managing them in one place — increasing efficiency and saving time.

## Primary Purpose
- **Security & Threat Detection** — complete visibility across the network to detect unauthorized access, suspicious behavior, malware activity, or policy violations in real time.
- **Incident Response & Digital Forensics** — acts as a reliable "audit trail"/black-box record to help analysts perform root-cause analysis, understand how a breach occurred, determine its scope, and rebuild timelines during an investigation.
- **Regulatory Compliance** — satisfies legal, regulatory, and industry mandates (e.g. PCI-DSS, HIPAA, ISO 27001) requiring organizations to store and protect audit logs for specified retention periods.
- **System Operations & Troubleshooting** — helps IT teams monitor infrastructure health, debug software errors, identify performance bottlenecks, and optimize system reliability.
