## Definition 
Malicious software that infiltrates servers, computers, and networks, encrypting valuable files so they become inaccessible. Attackers demand a ransom payment — often in cryptocurrency — for a decryption key.
## Real-world impact — WannaCry (May 2017) 
Spread across 150+ countries, affecting 200,000+ computers. UK's NHS was hard-hit — staff locked out of patient records, leading to canceled surgeries and diverted ambulances. Demanded $300–$600 in Bitcoin.
