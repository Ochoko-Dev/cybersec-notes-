# Definition 
Relies on **psychological manipulation** to deceive individuals into revealing confidential information or compromising security. Targets the weakest link in security: **the human**. 
## Five fundamental techniques 
1. Phishing 
2. Pretexting 
3. Baiting 
4. Tailgating 
5. Quid pro quo
