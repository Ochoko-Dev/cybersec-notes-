## Definition 
An organized group with specialized skills collaborating on cyberattacks — expert programmers, network specialists, social engineers, data analysts. 
## Notable role: Exfiltration Specialist 
Extracts data or deploys ransomware while evading detection, meticulously covering their tracks.
