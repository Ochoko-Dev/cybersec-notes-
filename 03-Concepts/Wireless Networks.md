## Core Mechanics
- **Wireless router** — forwards packets between networks; also acts as a Wi-Fi access point. Components: WAN, LAN, antennae, processor, memory.
- **Mobile hotspot** — broadcasts a cellular connection (4G/5G) as local Wi-Fi, using an internal modem + NAT to assign local IPs and route traffic. Available as phone tethering or standalone hardware (MiFi). 
## Practical Impact / Security Relevance
Operating a hotspot demands significant battery power, generates heat, and relies heavily on strong wireless encryption (WPA2/WPA3) to secure shared network data from unauthorized access.
